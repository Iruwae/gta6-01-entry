SOLMAR BAY — an original open-world crime game. Marco Solis is back in Solmar Bay with $120 and a debt; Lena Cruz at the Bayline garage has four jobs for a driver who asks no questions. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Bayline PD under a 5-star wanted system, fight the harbor crew, and pull the Big Score. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, everything runs from the static files under `game/`.

## How to play

- **WASD** move / drive · **Mouse** look · **Click** shoot · **E** enter & steal cars
- **Shift** sprint · **R** reload · **P** pause & save · **M** mute
- Follow the yellow beacon and the minimap: 4 story missions (First Wheels, Courier Run, Harbor Heat, The Big Score), traffic and pedestrians, police chases, combat, and a localStorage save.
